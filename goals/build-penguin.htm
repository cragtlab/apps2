01 / The 10x math

Followers are the output of a funnel, not a posting wish.

A 10x target is ambitious, not guaranteed. The controllable system is to create more qualified non-follower reach, make the profile instantly understandable, and turn curiosity into a follow.

Working equation New followers = reach × profile visits × follow conversion

Track the rates for every Reel. If reach rises but follows do not, fix the profile promise. If profile visits are low, fix the hook or the caption. If reach is low, fix the first second and the shareability.

  1. Reach people who do not follow youPrioritise a clean original premise, immediate visual contrast and a payoff worth sending to one specific person.
  2. Earn the profile visitUse recurring cover language and a caption that makes the viewer want the next example, not a generic “follow for more.”
  3. Make the profile pass in five secondsBio, pinned Reels and the latest nine posts should all explain the same Penguin Opposites promise.
  4. Convert with a seriesPeople follow when they can predict what they will get next. Number episodes and keep one open loop alive.
2/day

One discovery Reel plus one relationship Reel. Do not spend both slots on the same edit.

7 days

Enough time to compare a format family without changing every variable at once.

3x

Remake a winning idea three times with new footage or a new angle before retiring it.

10x

Use as a 180-day target and review point, not as a promise made to the algorithm.

02 / The creative engine

Own one repeatable contrast.

“Penguin Opposites” gives you a natural editorial device: show what viewers expect, then make the penguin, the science or the human meaning contradict it.

Lane 01 / cute · capable

Adorable does not mean passive.

Start with a cute behaviour, then reveal the surprising adaptation, skill or decision underneath it.

Hook: “This looks like a waddle. It is actually a survival system.”
Lane 02 / expectation · reality

Everyone thinks X. Watch Y.

Put the common assumption on screen for one beat, then let the clip or narration overturn it.

Hook: “You think the fastest penguin wins. The colony rewards something else.”
Lane 03 / tiny · huge

A small animal carries a big human truth.

Connect a real penguin moment to burnout, loyalty, awkwardness, ambition or friendship without pretending the animal has human motives.

Hook: “The most relatable workplace lesson is hiding in this penguin line.”
Lane 04 / fact · feeling

Give the fact a reason to matter.

Pair accurate natural history with an emotional payoff. Cite or verify science in the caption when the claim is specific.

Hook: “The fact is interesting. The reason people keep sharing it is personal.”
Originality guardrail: a saved Reel can show you the structure, but do not download and lightly re-caption it. Instagram says unoriginal content may be excluded from recommendations; use your own capture, licensed footage, a meaningful voiceover, or a genuinely new narrative.
03 / Turn saves into formats

Swipe the pattern, not the pixels.

For every saved Reel, extract the mechanism that made it work. Then create a Penguin Opposites version with a distinct hook, source, edit and payoff.

Step 01 / isolate

Write the hook in six words.

Ignore the creator’s caption. Describe the first-screen promise: the contradiction, question or visual surprise that stopped you.

Output: one sentence beginning “I need to know why…”
Step 02 / translate

Map the tension to a penguin.

Keep the emotional structure, not the surface subject. Turn “expectation vs reality” into a real behaviour, fact or observation from the penguin world.

Output: one verified premise and one visual proof.
Step 03 / rebuild

Make the first frame do work.

Open on the most legible movement or face. Add one large text line. Avoid an intro, logo animation or context paragraph before the contradiction.

Output: 3 first-frame variants for the same idea.
Step 04 / invite

Give the share a person.

Close with a reason to send: “send this to the friend who…” or a question that creates a real choice. Make the follow promise specific to the next episode.

Output: one share CTA and one series CTA.
04 / The two-Reel day

One Reel finds strangers. One makes them stay.

The schedule below creates 14 shots per week without requiring 14 unrelated creative ideas. Each day has a discovery job and a relationship job.

Monday / contrast

Make the account promise obvious.

  • Reach Reel“You thought penguins did X…” — fast contradiction with one visual proof.
  • Series ReelEpisode 01 of a weekly “Opposite of…” story with a comment prompt.
Tuesday / character

Give the audience someone to recognise.

  • Reach ReelA distinctive penguin behaviour with a human-readable, accurate caption.
  • Series Reel“Meet the opposite” — one recurring character, colony role or personality frame.
Wednesday / myth

Turn a misconception into a share.

  • Reach ReelMyth on frame one, correction by second three, proof before the final beat.
  • Series ReelReply to a real comment with a new example, not a screenshot of the comment.
Thursday / feeling

Make the fact emotionally useful.

  • Reach ReelA loopable moment that makes viewers think “that is me” without fake animal dialogue.
  • Series ReelVoiceover story: setup, opposite turn, honest takeaway, next-episode tease.
Friday / format test

Spend one slot on a controlled experiment.

  • Reach ReelTest a new hook or opening shot against the best-performing format family.
  • Series ReelRemake the week’s strongest idea with a new source and a different ending.
Saturday / community

Let the audience choose the next opposite.

  • Reach ReelTwo possible interpretations; ask viewers to pick A or B before the reveal.
  • Series ReelUse the winning comment to launch the next episode within 48 hours.
Sunday / compounding

Convert the week’s learning into a repeatable asset.

  • Reach ReelBest hook remake with new footage, not a duplicate upload.
  • Series Reel“Three opposites this week” recap with a direct reason to follow next week.
Low-time production rule: batch one theme, seven hooks and seven proof moments. Record voiceovers in one sitting, keep three caption templates, and publish the two daily jobs at separate times. Let Instagram Insights decide timing after two weeks of evidence.
05 / Fast production

Use templates to protect the creative brain.

A repeatable edit is not repetitive content. Keep the structure familiar while changing the observation, source and emotional turn.

Discovery Reel / 8–18 seconds to test 0–1s CONTRADICTION 1–5s VISUAL PROOF 5–10s TWIST OR LOOP LAST SEND TO ONE PERSON

Test the length that produces the strongest completion and shares for your audience. There is no magic duration; the opening must earn the next second.

Relationship Reel / 15–45 seconds to test SETUP “You expect…” TURN “But watch…” PAYOFF “That means…” NEXT “Episode __ tomorrow”

Use the second slot to establish voice, recurring series and reasons to follow. It can be slower if every line advances the story.

06 / Convert the visit

Make the profile explain itself before the viewer leaves.

Reach is wasted when the profile feels like a random animal clip page. The grid should make the next follow feel like an obvious decision.

Bio draft Penguins doing the opposite of expected 🐧 Real behaviour · strange facts · tiny stories New opposite twice a day ↓

Replace the wording with the account’s real promise. Keep the bio specific enough that a stranger can predict the next Reel.

  • Pin three entry-point ReelsWhat is Penguin Opposites? What is the funniest or most surprising example? What series should a new person start with?
  • Use covers as a menuOne repeated 2–4 word label per format: EXPECTED, OPPOSITE, FACT, EPISODE. Keep the subject visible.
  • Make the follow CTA a contract“Follow for tomorrow’s opposite” is stronger than “follow for more” because it names the next reward.
  • Check recommendation eligibilityUse a public professional account and review Account Status; eligibility enables recommendation but does not guarantee distribution.
07 / Sell like DCHTOONS

Build the catalogue while you build the audience.

The useful DCHTOONS benchmark is not a magic follower number. It is the sequence: relatable comics, a recognisable body of designs, low-priced impulse products, then keychains, clubs, markets and retail.

What the public case study shows

DCHTOONS says it began in 2021 as a comic account and evolved into a shop and community for sticker and keychain lovers.

A 2025 SUTD case study reports that Dylan posted comics for a year before stickers, started with 40 designs, grew toward almost 200, and saw a daily-video challenge create a major sales and follower inflection.

Inference: the repeatable asset is not “more posts.” It is a large enough original design library that viewers can recognise, collect and buy the characters.

If the audience converts, make the next 180 Reels around the winners; if it does not, change the product promise before manufacturing inventory.
08 / The 180-day scoreboard

Judge every Reel by the job it was hired to do.

Likes are not the main decision metric. Compare each Reel with the account’s own recent median and look for signals that create discovery or conversion.

Discovery Reel

Did strangers stop?

  • Non-follower reach
  • Average watch time / completion
  • Shares or sends per reach
Winner: 2x your recent median on reach quality, not just raw views.
Relationship Reel

Did they understand you?

  • Profile visits
  • Comments that name the idea
  • Follows per profile visit
Winner: people ask for the next episode or quote the series promise.
Weekly review

What gets repeated?

  • Top hook family
  • Best proof type
  • Best CTA and length range
Keep one variable stable and change one variable next week.
Day 180

Did the system compound?

  • Follower multiple vs day one
  • Repeatable series with demand
  • Owned library of original assets
If not 10x, keep the best format and reset the target from evidence.
Stop or pivot: if 30–40 Reels produce no format family above your median, do not simply post more. Change the promise, the first-frame contrast, the source quality or the audience. Do not buy followers or engineer fake engagement; it damages the signal you need to learn.
