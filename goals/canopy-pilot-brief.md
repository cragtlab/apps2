# Canopy Singapore pilot brief

**Status:** Draft for pre-consultation only · **Date:** 13 August 2026  
**Purpose:** Obtain written regulatory direction and a clinical partner’s view before any animal, sample, product or identifiable record is introduced.  
**Not:** An application, clinical protocol approval, treatment recommendation, manufacturing instruction or legal opinion.

## 1. Decision requested

Canopy is asking Singapore regulators, veterinary partners and scientific partners to answer one practical question:

> What is the smallest lawful, ethical and clinically useful feasibility programme through which a Singapore-based company can coordinate personalised canine oncology cases with qualified partners?

The preferred year-one outcome is a regulator-aligned feasibility pilot. It is **not** a promise to sell an unapproved personalised vaccine in Singapore within twelve months.

The first proposed product is the coordination and evidence layer:

- vet/referral intake and eligibility record;
- owner permission and research/data-consent versioning;
- sample identity and custody checkpoints;
- partner-run genomics and candidate-review hand-off;
- qualified manufacturing/QC hand-off where permitted;
- licensed veterinary treatment record and longitudinal outcome capture.

Canopy would not independently diagnose, select treatment, manufacture mRNA, release a biologic, administer treatment or replace a veterinarian.

## 2. Proposed staged scope

### Stage 0 — workflow and data feasibility

No animal treatment and no real owner data in the prototype. Use synthetic records and, only where the owner, institution and applicable approvals permit, de-identified historical records to test:

- clinic intake completeness;
- sample and data hand-off fields;
- turnaround and failure measurement;
- report review and sign-off responsibilities;
- outcome-schema usability;
- security, access and audit requirements.

This stage proves operations, not safety or efficacy.

### Stage 1 — partner and regulatory feasibility

Before prospective collection or treatment, obtain:

1. a named Singapore veterinary oncology/clinical lead;
2. a written genomics/pathology partner scope;
3. a written manufacturing/QC feasibility scope;
4. AVS-informed classification and import/possession questions answered in writing;
5. the appropriate ethics/IACUC determination if the activity is scientific use of animals;
6. owner-facing consent and data-governance materials reviewed by qualified counsel and the clinical partner.

No case is enrolled merely because a partner has signed an MOU. The permitted activity, responsible person and evidence attachment must be recorded per case.

### Stage 2 — permitted feasibility cases

Only if the responsible authority, ethics process, licensed veterinarian and qualified manufacturing partner permit it, run a small number of cases under the approved route. The programme should predefine:

- eligibility and exclusion criteria;
- who owns clinical decisions;
- sample acceptance/rejection and chain of custody;
- design-review sign-off;
- manufacturing/QC release responsibilities;
- storage, shipment and receipt controls;
- safety/adverse-event escalation;
- imaging/pathology and quality-of-life timepoints;
- data retention, withdrawal and research-use rules;
- stopping rules for the pilot.

Canopy will not improvise these items inside software or infer approval from owner consent.

### Public capability routes to validate

For partner discovery, Canopy has recorded public Singapore capability pages for [iCORE Genomics / GIS](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/icore/icore-platforms/core-platforms/icore-genomics---sequencing) (WES and genomic analytics), [AMPL / Translational Pathology Consortium](https://www.rsc.a-star.edu.sg/technologyplatforms/scientific-side-menu/scientific-information/national-shared-platform/translational-pathlogy-consortium) (veterinary pathology), [A*STAR BTI / NATi](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/bti/programmes-in-bti) (mRNA process development), [Hilleman Laboratories](https://hilleman-labs.org/platforms/) (Singapore RNA/biologics platform) and [BioNTech Singapore](https://www.biontech.com/int/en/home/mediaroom/news/press-releases/2022/11/biontech-expands-global-footprint-acquiring-gmp-manufacturing.html) (GMP mRNA facility). These are conversation-only routes checked 13 August 2026; none establishes access, canine suitability, qualification, availability, interest or approval. The next gate is a written scope and feasibility response.

## 3. Questions for AVS and qualified counsel

Ask for written guidance on the proposed facts, rather than assuming a website category applies to a personalised product.

### Product and activity classification

1. How should a patient-specific mRNA product for treatment of a privately owned dog be classified in Singapore?
2. Does each patient-specific sequence/formulation fall within veterinary biologics, vaccine, medicinal product or another category?
3. Which entity must hold the relevant licences: Canopy, the clinic, the manufacturer, the importer, or more than one party?
4. Is a software/orchestration company allowed to coordinate the workflow without possessing or importing the product?

### Possession, manufacture and import

5. For research/development, samples, field trials and finished treatment material, which AVS possession, registration, import and inspection steps apply?
6. If manufacture occurs outside Singapore, what documents are required for samples, bulk/unlabelled material and finished labelled material?
7. What storage, premises, cold-chain, consignment and release records must be maintained locally?
8. What is the correct route if a product is made patient-by-patient and there is no conventional catalogue product?

### Animal work and clinical care

9. Which proposed stages constitute scientific use of animals, field trial, product testing, diagnosis or treatment?
10. When is an institutional animal-care and use committee (IACUC) review required, and which institution should sponsor it?
11. What NACLAR, training, occupational-health and 3Rs obligations apply to the proposed work?
12. What must be done by an AVS-licensed veterinarian, and are any additional permissions required for inoculation or administration?
13. What adverse-event, treatment and outcome records must be reported or retained?

### Cross-border material and data

14. Can tumour/blood samples be sent to an overseas genomics partner, and what approvals, declarations or permits apply?
15. Can a finished product be imported for a permitted case, and who is the importer of record?
16. What data-transfer, owner-consent and retention requirements apply when a Singapore case is analysed or manufactured overseas?

**Record:** date, authority/contact, question wording, response, attached document, assumptions, owner and next action. A verbal answer is a prompt to request written confirmation, not a green light.

## 4. Clinical partner discussion

The first clinical conversation is a workflow interview, not a request to enrol patients. Ask the clinical lead to validate:

- the first narrow indication with enough case volume and interpretable outcomes;
- what tissue and matched healthy sample are already collected in ordinary care;
- the minimum clinic time and new steps;
- how eligibility, owner expectations and alternatives are explained;
- the acceptable design-review format;
- safety, imaging, pathology and quality-of-life checkpoints;
- what the clinic will and will not own;
- what would make the clinician stop the programme.

The clinical partner should be able to sign a letter that names responsibilities without approving a treatment or promising efficacy.

## 5. Measures for the year-one decision

| Gate | Required evidence | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|
| Month 2 | Named licensed clinical champion and signed workflow letter | No champion or no safe clinical owner |
| Month 3 | Written classification/pathway and ethics/IACUC plan | No credible lawful route |
| Month 4 | Genomics and manufacturing/QC scopes with quotes and responsibilities | No qualified partner-led route |
| Month 6 | Committed runway plus at least 10 qualified case leads | No funding or no eligible demand |
| Month 9 | Approved/permitted feasibility work with traceable safety/operations data | No permitted cases or no reproducible workflow |
| Month 12 | Unit economics, partner feedback, evidence review and board/founder decision | Stop therapeutic build or pivot to a separately validated workflow business |

“Qualified lead” means a case that a licensed clinical partner says may fit the defined indication and pathway. It does not mean a social-media follower or an owner promised treatment.

## 6. Guardrails

- Use synthetic data until a secure, governed production environment and consent process exist.
- Do not upload owner, dog or pathology information to this local prototype.
- Do not describe a single case or company claim as clinical proof.
- Do not manufacture, import, administer or recommend a product from this brief.
- Keep clinical judgment with the licensed veterinarian.
- Treat all funding, partner and regulatory statuses as unverified until supported by a signed document or written authority response.
- Preserve a stop decision; sunk cost is not evidence.
