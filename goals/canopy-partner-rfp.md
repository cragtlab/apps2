# Canopy partner RFP packets

**Status:** Draft for conversations and budgetary quotes · **Date:** 13 August 2026  
**Important:** This is not a request to begin treatment, receive identifiable clinical data, manufacture a product or bypass regulatory/ethics review. Please reply with capability, scope, assumptions, exclusions and a budgetary estimate only.

## Shared company brief

Canopy is a Singapore-focused workflow and evidence company exploring a partner-led personalised canine oncology programme. The software coordinates case intake, consent, sample custody, genomics review, manufacturing hand-off, veterinary care records and outcomes. Canopy does not independently diagnose, select treatment, manufacture mRNA, release biologics or administer treatment.

The year-one objective is a lawful, regulator-aligned feasibility pilot or a clean stop. The preferred operating model is Singapore headquarters with qualified local or regional partners for clinical care, genomics, manufacturing/QC, cold chain and regulatory/quality work.

Please identify the exact legal entity, site, licence/quality status, responsible technical lead, geography, and work you are authorised and insured to perform.

## Packet A — veterinary oncology / clinical partner

### Conversation request

We are requesting a 30-minute workflow interview first. We are not asking you to approve a treatment, enrol a dog or endorse a clinical claim.

### Questions

1. Which narrow canine oncology indication has sufficient referral volume and interpretable endpoints for a feasibility programme?
2. Which activities are ordinary care, and which would be new research, field-trial or product-testing activities?
3. What licensed-veterinarian, clinic, owner-consent and ethics/IACUC responsibilities would be required?
4. What sample collection, diagnosis, staging and monitoring steps are realistic without compromising care?
5. What information must be in a design-review report before a clinician could consider any next step?
6. What safety, imaging, pathology and quality-of-life measures are feasible?
7. What would make you reject a case or stop the programme?

### Requested response

- named clinical lead and clinic;
- indication recommendation and reasons;
- workflow map and clinic-time estimate;
- clinical, ethics and owner-communication requirements;
- proposed role boundaries;
- whether you would consider a non-binding letter of support after review.

### Non-binding clinical-partner letter template

> We confirm that [clinic/organisation] discussed Canopy’s proposed workflow on [date]. We are willing to continue a feasibility discussion concerning [indication], subject to clinical judgment, AVS/regulatory direction, ethics/IACUC requirements, owner consent, contracts, funding and partner qualification. This letter does not approve a treatment, enrol a patient, represent efficacy, or create an obligation to provide clinical care.

## Packet B — genomics / pathology partner

### Scope to quote

Provide a budgetary and technical response for a qualified partner to receive and process tumour tissue and a matched healthy sample, subject to the partner’s accepted sample requirements and applicable approvals. The requested output is a quality-controlled, reviewable data package for a clinician/scientific review—not an automatic treatment decision.

### Questions

1. What sample types, quantities, preservation, packaging and acceptance criteria do you support?
2. Can you support matched tumour/normal analysis and what quality controls define an acceptable run?
3. What assay and analysis scope do you recommend for a narrow canine oncology feasibility programme?
4. What are expected turnaround, failure/repeat rates and per-case costs?
5. What report fields, provenance, versioning and sign-off are supplied?
6. Who owns raw data, derived results, pipeline improvements and re-analysis rights?
7. Where are samples and data processed, stored and transferred?
8. What permits, biosafety, chain-of-custody and cross-border conditions apply?
9. What would you need from a veterinary clinic and a manufacturing partner?
10. Can you support de-identified/historical feasibility work before prospective cases?

## Packet C — mRNA design / manufacturing / QC partner

### Scope to quote

Provide a feasibility response for receiving a controlled, clinician-reviewed design package and determining whether patient-specific mRNA production, formulation, quality testing, storage and shipment could be supported under the applicable quality and regulatory route.

This request deliberately does **not** prescribe a manufacturing method or ask for experimental instructions. The partner must define the lawful and technically appropriate process, controls and release criteria.

### Questions

1. What patient-specific mRNA or RNA-therapeutic capability do you currently support, and at what development stage?
2. What quality system, site status, batch documentation and release testing can you provide?
3. What input design package, sequence controls, specifications and approvals are required?
4. What are the realistic development lead time, manufacturing lead time, minimum quantity and per-case cost?
5. What identity, integrity, purity, sterility/endotoxin, formulation and stability evidence would be included in a release package, as appropriate to the product and route?
6. Can you support small-batch, patient-specific work, and how do you manage deviations, failures and rework?
7. What storage, label, cold-chain, shipment and receipt controls are available?
8. Which party is responsible for product classification, registration, import, release and administration permissions?
9. Can feasibility start with a non-clinical or non-treatment assessment before any animal case?
10. What information may be shared with Canopy, the clinic, genomics partner and regulator under the proposed data/IP arrangement?

## Packet D — quality, regulatory and security advisor

Request a scoped review covering:

- Singapore product/activity classification questions for AVS;
- possession, import, registration, sample and field-trial routes;
- NACLAR/IACUC applicability;
- veterinary licence and clinic role boundaries;
- cross-border sample/data controls;
- consent, withdrawal, retention and incident response;
- quality-system and vendor qualification plan;
- the exact evidence required before the pilot gates can be marked `Met`.

Ask for assumptions and exclusions in writing. A memo that says “consult AVS” without a question list, facts and decision log is not sufficient for Gate 2.

## Shared partner-selection scorecard

Score 0–3 and record evidence:

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Relevant canine / RNA capability | None | Adjacent | Demonstrated | Direct, current capability |
| Quality and traceability | Unknown | Informal | Documented | Auditable and partner-owned |
| Regulatory clarity | Cannot answer | General answer | Written assumptions | Written path/owner/referral |
| Turnaround and capacity | Unknown | Uncertain | Quoted | Quoted with capacity evidence |
| Data/IP/consent fit | Unclear | Needs work | Contractable | Clear and acceptable |
| Cross-border logistics | None | Possible | Quoted | Documented with responsible importer |
| Safety/clinical boundaries | Blurred | Partly defined | Defined | Explicit, licensed owner |

**Do not select a partner on scientific enthusiasm alone.** Require a written scope, named owner, assumptions, exclusions, price range, capacity and evidence attachment.
