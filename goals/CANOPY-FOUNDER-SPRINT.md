# Canopy first-30-day founder sprint

**Purpose:** turn the Singapore personalised canine-oncology concept into external evidence quickly.

**Boundary:** this sprint prepares documents and conversations. It does not enrol an animal, collect identifiable records, manufacture a product, submit an application, send outreach automatically or claim clinical efficacy.

The live MVP tracks these actions under **Investor proof → First 30-day evidence sprint**. Mark an action `Done` only when its local artifact exists. A website, an unreturned message or a verbal possibility is not completion.

| Due | Action | Owner | Required artifact | Gate affected |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| Day 3 | Narrow indication brief | Founder + veterinary lead | One-page indication, eligibility, exclusion, endpoint and stop-rule brief | Clinical champion |
| Day 7 | Clinical workflow interview packet | Founder | Interview script, named questions and non-binding support-letter template | Clinical champion |
| Day 10 | AVS/counsel question log | Founder + QA/counsel | Facts, questions, owner, date, written response and attachment fields | Regulatory / ethics |
| Day 14 | Genomics/manufacturing packets | Scientific lead | Partner-ready sample/QC and mRNA/QC/cold-chain/data/IP quote packets | Manufacturing route |
| Day 14 | Investor one-pager | Founder / investor lead | Thesis, wedge, team gaps, 12-month gates, planning budget and caveats | Funding |
| Day 21 | Company/IP readiness | Founder + counsel/CSP | Incorporation decision, IP assignments, invention log, data-rights schedule and data-room index | Regulatory / financing |
| Day 21 | Synthetic MVP review | Founder + technical lead | Five-minute demo, smoke-test output, case-free snapshot and product-gap list | Regulatory / financing |
| Day 30 | Evidence review | Founders | Completed-artifact review, external responses, blockers and continue/stop/pivot recommendation | Clinical / year-one decision |

## Day-30 decision rule

Continue only if the founder has a credible clinical conversation or named clinical lead, a regulator/counsel question log with an owned next step, and partner-ready genomics/manufacturing packets. If any critical path has no owner or no plausible route, mark the relevant action `Blocked` and record a stop/pivot recommendation rather than extending the plan by optimism.

The year-one hard gates remain:

- Month 2: named clinical champion and role-boundary letter;
- Month 3: written classification/regulatory/ethics path;
- Month 4: qualified manufacturing/QC feasibility route;
- Month 6: committed runway plus qualified case demand;
- Month 9: permitted, reproducible pilot evidence;
- Month 12: written scale-or-stop decision.

## What counts as external evidence

- a named person and dated meeting record;
- a written eligibility or pathway response;
- a signed non-binding support letter with explicit exclusions;
- a written partner scope, quote or quality document;
- a financing term or committed runway document;
- a permitted protocol and traceable pilot records.

The MVP/API are development-only and memory/local-storage based. Keep all records synthetic until the production security, privacy, veterinary, regulatory and ethics requirements are satisfied.
