CANOPY/INVESTOR PACK
Working MVP  ·  One-year plan
Singapore / pre-seed handoff / 13 Aug 2026

Make personalised canine oncology repeatable across Asia.

Canopy is a partner-led workflow and evidence company: it coordinates veterinary intake, sample custody, genomics review, qualified manufacturing and longitudinal outcomes so a personalised treatment programme can scale without every clinic inventing its own process.

01 / investor story

Ten slides, one honest claim.

Use this as the skeleton for a deck. Every number or clinical statement must be replaced with a source, a partner letter or Canopy’s own measured evidence.

01

Title

Canopy — the Singapore and Southeast Asia partner layer for personalised canine cancer treatment.

02

Problem

Veterinary oncology cases are fragmented across clinic intake, pathology, genomics, manufacturing, cold chain and outcome monitoring. A clinic cannot safely scale a bespoke programme through email and spreadsheets.

03

Why now

Gamgee’s public workflow demonstrates a clear category reference: tumour-derived personalised mRNA treatment, veterinary referral, genomic analysis, clinician review, partner manufacturing and monitoring. Canopy starts with the regional coordination gap.

04

Product

Structured intake and consent, sample chain of custody, versioned analysis review, manufacturing hand-off, clinical event log and an investor-grade evidence dashboard. The working MVP demonstrates these states with synthetic data.

05

Technical moat

Longitudinal, consent-backed data linking diagnosis, sample quality, tumour/normal evidence, candidate review, batch/QC, treatment and response. The moat is traceability and outcomes—not a generic dashboard or an unvalidated AI score.

06

Clinical and regulatory plan

Licensed veterinary partner owns care; qualified genomics and manufacturing partners own their regulated scopes; Canopy obtains an AVS-informed pathway before a Singapore pilot and follows the required ethics process.

07

Business model

Clinic or programme fee for case coordination and evidence services, with partner costs passed through transparently. Long term: recurring clinic platform + programme operations + consented research/data partnerships, subject to contracts and approvals.

08

Go-to-market

One Singapore oncology champion, referral clinics, regional case intake and an Australian bridge partner if Singapore volume is initially insufficient. Start with a narrow indication and a small number of high-information cases.

09

Milestones

M1 pathway and champion · M3 partner scopes and pilot brief · M6 financing and qualified leads · M9 permitted pilot evidence · M12 scale or stop decision.

10

Ask

Raise S$1.5–2.5m as an internal planning range to reach the next evidence milestone, subject to partner quotes. Ask investors for introductions to clinical, genomics, manufacturing and regulatory partners as well as capital.

06 / discipline

Stop cleanly if the evidence does not arrive.

Run this as a one-year option. The company earns another year only by hitting the gates below.

Stop or pivot the therapeutic build

No credible regulatory path by month 3; no clinical champion by month 2; no partner-led manufacturing route by month 4; no qualified cases by month 6; no committed runway by month 6; or no reproducible, safe, permitted workflow evidence by month 9. Preserve the workflow/data product only if clinics will pay for it and the narrower business has independent evidence.
