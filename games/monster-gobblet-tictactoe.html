Monster Gobblet

SIMPLE OPERATION, EASY TO USE — big monsters gulp the small ones. Line up 3 to win!
Red's turn
How to play: Each monster has 3 sizes — small, medium, large (2 of each). Tap a monster from your tray, then tap a cell to place it. A bigger monster can gulp any smaller monster already on that cell (yours or theirs) — the small one hides underneath until uncovered. Get 3 of your color showing in a row, column, or diagonal to win.
