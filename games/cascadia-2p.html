🏔️ Cascadia — Pass & Play

A two-player, single-page adaptation of the tile-drafting habitat game. Draft a habitat tile & wildlife token, grow your own Pacific Northwest, and see who builds the richest ecosystem.

Habitat corridors

At game end, each player finds their largest connected group of each terrain type (mountain, forest, prairie, wetland, river). A group of size n scores 1+2+…+n points, capped and then growing more slowly past 7 tiles. Whoever has the single largest corridor of a given terrain (ties share it) earns a +2 bonus for that terrain.

Wildlife, each with its own personality

  • 🐻 Bears like to den in exactly a pair — a connected group of exactly two bears scores big. Loners or crowds of three+ score nothing.
  • 🦌 Elk like company in a line — longer connected herds score steadily more, up to a cap.
  • 🐟 Salmon want long unbroken runs — the longer the connected run, the faster the points climb, uncapped.
  • 🦅 Hawks want solitude — a hawk with no other hawk touching it scores; each isolated hawk adds more than the last.
  • 🦊 Foxes like diverse neighbors — each fox scores one point per distinct species of wildlife adjacent to it.

Nature tokens

Placing wildlife on a "keystone" tile (one that only accepts a single species) earns a nature token. Spend one to mix-and-match any tile with any token instead of a paired draft, or to reroll all four market wildlife tokens. Unspent tokens are worth 1 point each at game end.
